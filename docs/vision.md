# Vision

## Core Thesis

The golden era of Indian film music produced extraordinary work under constraints that demanded mastery — single-take analog recording, live orchestras, classically-trained composers (Naushad, Madan Mohan, S.D. Burman, Roshan), and singers who could deliver technically demanding classical-based pieces flawlessly under pressure. That ecosystem no longer exists, and the music risks fading from cultural memory as generations move on.

Alaap is a preservation and appreciation project that makes this music accessible, discoverable, and understandable — filling a gap no existing resource covers end-to-end.

## Three Pillars

1. **Structured Knowledge Base** — songs, ragas, composers, singers, films, eras, taals — seeded from existing data sources and enriched through manual curation
2. **Rich Contextual Layer** — what makes a composition remarkable: recording stories, classical roots, musical analysis, cultural context
3. **Exploratory Interface** — navigate by raga, composer, era, mood, complexity, influence — not just film or popularity. Includes guided pathways for new listeners.

## Era Scope

- **Core**: Late 1940s through end of 1970s
- **Soft boundary** — musical DNA matters more than strict dates. A soundtrack like *Lekin* (1990, Hridaynath Mangeshkar composing for Lata) counts because it carries the golden era sensibility. The filter is the music's classical rigor, not the calendar.
- **Phased approach**: Start with the core era, expand to outliers as the knowledge base grows.

## What's Missing Today

Existing resources each serve one axis well but leave the others untouched:

| Resource | Strength | Gap |
|----------|----------|-----|
| **HindiGeetMala** | Comprehensive database (40-60K songs) | No soul — data without context |
| **MrAndMrs55** | Scholarly depth, cultural context | Narrow coverage |
| **Raag Hindustani** | Classical theory education | Doesn't connect to film music |
| **Planet Bollywood** | Curated canons | No musical analysis |
| **Rohit Kataria (YouTube)** | Raga analysis with notation | Video-only, not searchable |
| **70mm with Rahoul (YouTube)** | Oral history, storytelling | Not structured |

**No single project combines** structured data + raga analysis + contextual narrative + exploratory discovery. That's the gap Alaap fills.

## Design Principles

- **Graph-native** — relationships between entities (song↔raga, composer↔influence, raga↔thaat) are first-class. The graph database is the foundation, not an afterthought.
- **Accessible to the uninitiated** — you shouldn't need classical training to appreciate why a composition is extraordinary. The interface should bridge that gap.
- **Personal** — this project exists because of one person's deep love for this music. That personal voice (Praneet's favorites, personal annotations) is what gives it soul.
- **Iterative** — MVP first, then layer on. Don't over-commit to patterns early. Build the simplest thing that works, then adapt.

## Inspirations

### Websites
- [MrAndMrs55](https://mrandmrs55.com/) — scholarly song translations, cultural context, linguistic education
- [Raag Hindustani](https://raag-hindustani.com/) — pedagogical approach to classical fundamentals with dual notation
- [HindiGeetMala](https://www.hindigeetmala.net/) — comprehensive Hindi film song database
- [Planet Bollywood 100 Greatest Soundtracks](https://planetbollywood.com/displayArticle.php?id=060306044135) — curated canon

### YouTube Channels
- [Rohit Kataria](https://www.youtube.com/@RohitKataria) — 200K+ subscribers. Classically trained (PhD candidate, 4x UGC NET). Analyzes Hindi film songs through ragas with notation.
- [70mm with Rahoul](https://www.youtube.com/@70mmwithRahoul) — Bollywood nostalgia, trivia, oral history. Interviews artists about the stories behind their creations.
